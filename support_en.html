About Us

HANA JUNGJUN TECH aims to connect people, technology, and precious memories. 'Travel Diary' is the first footprint born from that vision, helping travelers visualize their world journey in a beautiful way.

Behind the Scenes (TMI)

I started this app simply because I love traveling and wanted a way to keep my own records. While I spend many nights battling servers and occasionally get flustered seeing the Antarctica map turn bright red, I'm having a great time developing this project thanks to your amazing support.
