About Us

HANA JUNGJUN TECH is a project-based team focusing on blending technology with daily experiences. "Travel Diary" is our latest passion project designed to help travelers around the world keep their memories forever.

Behind the Scenes

Developing this app started with a simple question: "How can I record my trips more vividly?" It has been a long journey of coding and debugging, but seeing travelers use the app makes it all worth it. We are constantly working to improve and add new features!
